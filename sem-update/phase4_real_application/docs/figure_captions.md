# Figure captions and evidence types

**A — Process model and randomized total effects.** Panel a shows the complete
11-node training-only initialization and returned M3 graph at common physical-role
positions. Blue/orange/gray identify retained/added/removed model edges. Dashed
returned edges occur in fewer than three of five grouped fitting-acquisition
refits; this is selection frequency, not causal probability. Directions come
from the fitted DAG, not the layout. Panel b reports measured high-minus-low
effects under randomized actuator assignments: colors use fitting SDs and cell
numbers use amperes or ambient-relative pascals. Effects are averaged equally
over repeated primary acquisition files for each actuator. Total-effect evidence
does not validate any particular direct model edge. Sources: graphs.json,
edge_frequency.csv, plot_total_effects.csv and observed_effects.csv.

**B — Recorded versus generated intervention responses.** The pressure and current
panels use the prespecified first-named inlet/outlet validation acquisitions and
the ten-second hatch acquisition. Black points and bars are empirical condition
means and 95% ten-row-block bootstrap intervals; colored dashed lines connect
predicted means at the two recorded assignments. Connections do not represent
measurements at intermediate commands. Each predicted condition uses 4,096 draws.
The empirical bars are confidence intervals, not individual-outcome predictive
intervals. Separate 90% model predictive bounds are retained in plot_responses.csv
and coverage/width metrics. Sources: plot_responses.csv and prediction manifests.

**C — Actuator-by-outcome effects.** The measured, ridge, M3 and M5 panels use the
same color scale and fitting-derived standardization. Three actuator targets and
five prespecified engineering outcomes are displayed. Measured changes use the
same fixed-nuisance contrasts as A; model changes use the corresponding complete
intervention distributions. Mixture predictions do not imply an averaged causal
DAG. Source: plot_total_effects.csv.

**D — Accuracy and charged computation.** Primary contrast error is averaged
equally over engineering outcomes and actuator targets. Intervals are paired
acquisition/block bootstrap intervals conditional on this apparatus and fixed
fitted models; the hatch target has only one acquisition. M3–M5 each pay for their
whole shared candidate bank. The logarithmic time axis shows standalone fitting
and selection costs; inference, discovery/refit and shared execution costs are
reported separately. M2 denotes capped/projected DCDI-DSF adaptation plus common
flows. No formal superiority is inferred from the visually lowest point. Sources:
summary.csv and costs.csv.

**E — Recorded pressure/current alternatives.** Points show measured means for
the low and high arms in each primary randomized acquisition. Horizontal and
vertical bars are empirical block-bootstrap confidence intervals. Larger points
denote high assignments. Dotted connectors identify two recorded alternatives,
not a measured continuous frontier. The dashed pressure target, 6.1875 Pa, was
fixed from development data. No power, airflow or optimal-controller claim is
made. Sources: plot_tradeoff.csv, decisions.csv and protocol.json.

**Supplement — M5 component graphs.** Each complete component is shown separately,
at the same positions as A, with its predictive combination weight. Weights are
fixed for all final interventions and do not express causal graph truth. Full
PDF/SVG/PNG variants and component graphs are preserved in ignored artifacts.

Every figure is generated by `tools/render_figures.py`. Compact plotting tables
are sufficient to reproduce the final graphics without raw observations, model
checkpoints or CUDA. Raster previews are rendered from the scientific vectors;
no image-generation model is used for charts.
