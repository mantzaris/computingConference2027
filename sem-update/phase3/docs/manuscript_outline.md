# Manuscript integration outline

This outline specifies how to integrate the executed phases. It is not a final
manuscript, a submission, or evidence of Alex's approval. The executed scaling
narrative is: observed M3 accuracy at 20/50 nodes, failure to repair at 100,
conservative M5 reliability with small large-graph gains, metric dilution,
and incomplete strict DCDI convergence. All claims trace to the completed
phase-three evidence report and claim ledger.

1. **Problem and scope.** Define a generative SCM with independent disturbances,
   perfect known-target interventions and conditional-flow mechanisms. State the
   prediction endpoint before discussing graph recovery. Graph proposals and
   predictive success do not establish causal truth. Motivate repair of an
   imperfect supplied graph under a finite fitting budget.
2. **Related work.** Distinguish DCDI, causal normalizing flows, CMA, ABAPC-LLM
   and CauScientist. The LLM proposal/verification loop already exists, and DCDI
   already reports 100-node experiments. Present robust selection and anchored
   prediction as proposed extensions, with the narrow empirical scope in
   `positioning.md`; do not invent a priority or identification claim.
3. **Mechanisms and shared search.** Specify masks, topological generation,
   likelihood exclusions, independent noise, unchanged-mechanism caching and
   canonical parent-set seeds. Describe diagnostic scheduling separately from
   the common selection score. Explain the immutable M3–M5 bank, calibration,
   one-time audit, G0 fallback and whole-observation mixture sampling.
4. **Experimental design.** Preserve the distinction between the original
   controlled-corruption study, phase-two fresh controlled/semantic/real panels
   and phase-three fresh scale/profile systems. State independent SCM counts,
   target coverage, every non-test partition, training standardization, T1/T2/T3,
   numerical rejection rules, corruption, resource ceilings and oracle subset.
   Explain why conditions and generated observations are not independent SCMs.
5. **Six-method results and cost.** Present observed rankings with paired SCM
   uncertainty, actual method coverage and prespecified multiplicity control.
   Show each size separately. Include descendants, non-descendant invariance and
   true-response magnitude alongside joint SW1. Distinguish discovery from
   common-flow refitting, and logical method cost from shared device use.
6. **Reliability and structural complexity.** Retain the frozen harm definition.
   Report severity, G0 retention, worst-regime error and adverse examples.
   Compare fixed versus adjusted banks and matched update/time prefixes without
   claiming that one candidate DAG has constant cost. Describe dense, hub and
   deep profiles using measured topology, including the deliberately greater
   edge count of the dense profile. Report DCDI residuals and projection, not
   just the validity of its returned DAG.
7. **Earlier semantic and real evidence.** Preserve the negative findings and
   the observed ridge advantage on the real polarizer regime. One apparatus,
   exposed observations and exploratory regimes do not support a universal
   real-world ranking. No physical variables were duplicated to manufacture a
   large real benchmark. The phase-two functioning LLM adaptation remains a
   separate comparison; phase three supplies no new LLM-scale evidence.
8. **Limits and reproducibility.** Address five systems per cell, Monte Carlo
   error, finite search, metric dilution, baseline convergence, grouping and
   concurrency costs, generator assumptions, missing coverage and optional
   analyses actually completed. Describe artifact preservation, exact hashes,
   locked dependencies, frozen evaluation and human scientific responsibility.

Use the comparison/reliability tables and the size, computation, complexity,
harm, response and graph figures selectively in the main text. A whole-graph
overview shows structure; the declared common-layout local view supports edge
inspection. Put detailed coverage, convergence, sensitivity, per-system points
and extra graph panels in the allowed appendix. Never label predictive intervals
as confidence intervals, mixture weights as edge probabilities, or selected
adverse illustrations as representative averages.

The official format currently permits 18 main pages and up to seven additional
reference/appendix pages; the abstract is 150–250 words. An anonymous draft and
the required GenAI disclosure need human review before any submission. Disclose
AI assistance with implementation, execution support, analysis tooling and text
drafting accurately. Do not imply that a human has validated assumptions,
claims or final text until Alex has actually done so. The target deadline is
15 October 2026 AoE, subject to the official rules linked in the protocol.
