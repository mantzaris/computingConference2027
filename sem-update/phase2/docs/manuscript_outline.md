# Manuscript outline for Alex's review

Working title: *Selecting and Combining Repaired Structural Equation Models
for Intervention Prediction*. This is an outline, not an approved manuscript.
Use the completed phase-two tables, claim ledger and
[interpretation](interpretation.md) as its quantitative anchors.

1. **Problem and scope.** A plausible supplied DAG can predict interventions
   poorly. LLM suggestions are fallible prior information. Intervention-scored
   graph refinement, causal flows, robust prediction criteria and predictive
   stacking have established antecedents; do not claim their invention.
2. **Historical motivation.** Preserve the phase-one controlled improvement,
   unequal achieved fitting costs, ten harmful conditions after audit, adverse
   semantic/real results and broken LLM-edit interface. Explain why fresh systems
   and a separate follow-up protocol are necessary. Do not pool both phases to
   create a larger confirmatory sample.
3. **SCM implementation and six methods.** Specify independent disturbances,
   parent masks, shared intact mechanisms, perfect-intervention replacement and
   masked likelihoods. Define empirical M0, ridge M1, official DCDI-DSF plus
   common flows M2, calibration-average repair M3, robust selection M4 and the
   anchored predictive mixture M5. Distinguish mixture distributions from DAGs.
4. **Information and fitting controls.** Diagram fit/early/search/calibration/
   audit/final roles, the immutable bank, canonical parent-set cache and audit
   fallback. Explain why M3/M4/M5 share fits and each pays their logical cost.
   DCDI receives data and intervention targets but not the corrupted prior;
   this information difference limits comparisons of discovery and repair.
5. **Frozen evaluation.** Describe 15 new controlled SCMs, factorial budgets and
   corruption, five separate semantic SCMs, metadata controls and the real
   polarizer/RGB distinction. State all measured intervention budgets, including
   randomized reference-policy observations. Report hardware, dependencies,
   revisions, timings, failed attempts and actual coverage.
6. **Primary prediction and costs.** Use the six-method table, family/budget
   plot, paired SCM uncertainty and corrected comparisons. Separate descriptive
   best means from evidence of superiority. Show discovery and mechanism costs
   separately, and give accuracy/time tradeoffs rather than an arbitrary utility
   score. Include strict-convergence sensitivity for capped DCDI runs.
7. **Reliability.** Compare harmful frequency, severity, worst-regime error,
   mean gain and G0 retention jointly. Include rho=0, tau=0, uniform mixture,
   fixed-flow and random-priority controls. Show matched update/time checkpoints
   with their coverage. Include an adverse example if one occurs.
8. **Semantic/real panels and LLM diagnosis.** Show all relevant methods,
   including the functioning menu-edit adaptation. Report proposal-validation
   and verifier outcomes separately. New polarizer regimes are a limited
   one-apparatus follow-up; RGB remains exploratory. Conditional intervals use
   contiguous reporting blocks within one acquisition per regime; they do not
   quantify independent-acquisition or independent-apparatus uncertainty.
9. **Limits and conclusions.** Five SCMs per family, finite calibration data,
   bounded search/optimization, a small graph class, one frozen LLM and one
   physical apparatus limit generalization. Predictive success does not prove
   graph truth. State unsupported or adverse hypotheses explicitly. Present
   extensions as proposed procedures, with no priority or causal guarantee.
