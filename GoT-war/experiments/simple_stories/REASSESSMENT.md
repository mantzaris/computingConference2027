# Reassessment of the archived simple-story generations

The historical **0/6 is strict compiler acceptance**, not a finding that six outputs contained no useful reasoning. All six first attempts are parseable JSON, and all identify some relevant events and relationships. Controlled Humpty states all three reference rules correctly in its joint-mechanism prose; controlled Wolf states all four; controlled Crow states three of four. These are assistant semantic judgments against the supplied controlled texts, not independent researcher adjudication. None of the six is a complete, accepted executable model.

This post hoc review preserves [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md), [COMPARISON.tsv](COMPARISON.tsv), all six frozen texts/references and every request, response, repair and earlier audit in [records.json.xz](records.json.xz). It adds no inference. The original generator was Qwen3-14B-AWQ. Reassessment, proposed wording changes and publication presentation are by Codex. Independent review remains **pending**.

**Humpty.** The controlled output's “A repair succeeds exactly when Humpty is damaged, the helpers attempt repair, and an effective method is available” accurately captures the added rule. `S` versus reference `R` for success, and `Hd` versus `Y` for remaining damage, are harmless aliases. All six factual values are correct. However, one edge says an attempt is possible only when Humpty is already damaged: S5 concerns **successful repair**, while S7 makes the attempt independently settable. That is a real distinction to correct, not a synonym. The natural output successfully separates fall, initial damage, attempt and failure, but changes the endpoint to *permanent* irreparability and introduces an “irreversible damage” fact. Neither follows from the failed helpers. It also uses unspecified material/techniques as physical causes of irreversibility, puts those purported causes later than their effect, and labels all actors “Narrative.” It does not assume an egg. Smallest usable controlled revision: recover the interface, encode the already-correct three joint rules, clarify the erroneous attempt edge, record rule origins and staged times. Natural Humpty needs substantive scope/variable revision, with irreversible damage retracted and alternative repair left unresolved.

**Wolf.** The natural output provides a useful account of credibility: “The boy's false warnings caused the villagers to lose trust in his warnings.” It separates a new warning from disbelief and absence of help, with the correct boy, villagers and wolf; it invents no death. Its `wolf_attack_possible_stop` is null, preserving a useful uncertainty about hypothetical protection. The supplied mechanisms nevertheless stop short of linking dismissal to the no-help variable; absence of help remains a root. This is an incomplete end-to-end explanation, not evidence that the existing links are meaningless. Its generated `necessary` tags strengthen narrative descriptions beyond what their prose or source establishes. The controlled descriptions correctly express distrust, warning, response and harm, but `villagers_respond_in_time=1` contradicts S2/S5. This is the one wrong physical observation; it also conflicts with the response and harm rules. Smallest controlled revision: explicitly correct that value to zero, encode the four already-stated rules, and fix the interface and times. The natural version needs an explicit scoped treatment of its null root and review of the missing response link; no guaranteed protective effect may be added from memory.

**Crow.** The natural generation captures the physical sequence and distinguishes raised water from subsequent reach and drinking. “As the crow drops stones into the pitcher, the water level rises due to displacement” is a useful qualitative explanation; no numerical hydrodynamics is required. All eight scored factual assertions are supported, with the crow and pitcher correctly identified and the main sequence temporally ordered. Its leaking-pitcher gap is retained. `outcome="drank"` does not name the otherwise plausible `crow_drinks` node, and three declared endogenous variables lack mechanisms. Choosing the obvious drinking alias still requires a recorded interpretive mapping; the adapter does not guess it. In the controlled output, the reach rule correctly distinguishes initial reachability from water raised by stones. The adding-stones description incorrectly requires an intact pitcher in addition to available stones, contrary to S3. For `do(I=0,S=1)`, that proposed rule gives addition zero while the reference gives one; both final drinking outcomes remain zero. Thus this is an intermediate-mechanism error, not evidence that it changes every frozen outcome answer. The main rise/reach descriptions retain the intact-pitcher qualification; abbreviated edge phrases should be clarified in that context rather than all being counted as separate universal-sufficiency errors. Smallest controlled revision: remove the extra prerequisite in an explicitly assisted model, preserve “enough” in the addition definition, encode the four rules, attach ambiguous edges through recorded interpretation, and qualify short labels. Natural completion requires more than an identifier fix; unknown behavior must remain unknown.

## Normalization and partial execution

[reassessment.json](reassessment.json) records hashes, every relocation, metadata precedence operation, unresolved attachment, compiler diagnostic, compatible-family size and query result. The new general adapter does not contain story-specific extraction rules.

* Lossless relocation: attach a flat edge only if its supplied parent occurs in exactly one declared child mechanism, or it explicitly specifies a valid child. Attach a claim by a unique exact prose anchor; this locates the assertion and says nothing about its semantic truth. Preserve unresolved records. **28/35 flat edge records** can be attached this way; seven remain ambiguous. All **20** flat claim records have unique anchors. The mechanisms already declare **39** parent–child dependencies, including four natural-Crow dependencies without edge prose. Relocation does not establish that an edge's prose agrees with its structurally determined destination (natural Humpty contains a mismatch).
* Declared precedence, separately logged: nine children marked `fixed_context` also have explicit mechanisms. The derived audit view uses those mechanisms and marks the children endogenous. This resolves contradictory metadata by a stated convention; it is not labelled a lossless edit of an already consistent model.
* No semantic completion: no parent set, observation, equation, row constraint, source passage, or mechanism description is changed. Unknown outcome IDs, unknown roots and missing equations are not guessed. The original files remain untouched.

## Language, formal claims and genuine contradictions

Semantic assessment accepts equivalent terminology and meanings; it does not require reference-sentence matching. “Helps,” “enables” and factual “leads to” are not automatically translated into necessity, universal sufficiency or monotonicity. Ambiguous conditional phrases are read with their joint-mechanism context and flagged for clarification. For example, controlled Crow's edge “Water rises when stones are added” should read **“Added stones raise water in the intact pitcher”**; its main mechanism already says both are required. If the edge were intended as an unqualified universal, it would contradict the source. This review records that ambiguity instead of asserting a demonstrated second wrong mechanism. The same applies to its two short reachability phrases. The earlier four-item source-issue count is preserved historically but is not a count of four independent substantive mistakes.

For explicit exact claims, the archived assistant's formal translations can be checked against every local table without completing the generated model. Of the 11 controlled joint descriptions, **nine are compatible but not entailed** by the partial encoding. **Two are inconsistent** with every compatible local function: Wolf response and harm, because of the wrong response observation. The distinction matters: existence of one counterexample in an underspecified family is not proof that the intended prose rule is false. [reassessment.json](reassessment.json) retains passing-table counts and concrete counterexamples. These translations remain assistant judgments; exact quotations anchor them but do not certify their meaning.

No original model is silently repaired. Suggested editorial changes above are not applied to archived prose. Structural normalization is reproducible and logged; the minimal substantive corrections are recommendations, **not newly scored assisted models**. The strict all-configuration necessity/conjunction/inhibition checks and four historical mutation tests remain unchanged. This review neither relaxes a true formal assertion nor treats every informal phrase as one.
