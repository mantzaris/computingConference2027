# Three simple stories: frozen introductory checks

Freeze: 2026-10-13T19:10:34Z. `frozen.json` SHA-256: `12d1e7c00fd59dd570d94edf83d7b707c3975678f5b30367c4c657152859b302`.

This is a three-story development exercise, not a new benchmark campaign or evidence of general narrative understanding. The six original texts are 109–141 words each. Numbered sentences distinguish retelling from deliberately added rules. The reference models, questions and gold answers were written by Codex as experiment author and frozen before any generation. They are specifications of controlled adaptations, not independently established traditional-story interpretations. Humpty is not identified as an egg; helpers' failure does not prove universal irreparability. No boy's death is asserted. The crow model is qualitative and holds sufficient water and readiness to drink fixed.

Each controlled model has six or seven Boolean variables. Four questions per story comprise two individual interventions, one joint intervention, and a state-changing intervention that preserves the outcome. The crow's final question changes initial reachability from false to true; it is not merely resetting an input to its factual value. All questions and reference answers remain fixed. The natural versions each have a frozen question for which the text does not identify the answer.

## Procedure and limits

Use the existing cached Qwen3-14B-AWQ revision and project endpoint, with no thinking, temperature 0.2 and recorded seeds. Maximum: nine first requests (six constructions and three direct-answer baselines), at most three additional repairs (one per failed controlled construction), 40,000 generated tokens and one hour of service time including startup. End earlier on completion. No sweep, new infrastructure or paid API. The watchdog only controls identity-verified processes registered for this experiment. Raw logs and previews remain ignored. If inference fails, publish reference/solver checks and mark generation comparisons unrun.

Construction requests receive only the relevant numbered story, its outcome scope and the same general schema/instructions. No reference variable, equation, graph, intervention answer, alignment or scoring code is supplied. Baselines receive the same controlled story and the four question wordings, without a constructed graph. Requests are independent. Each exact request and first raw output is checkpointed before parsing; failed responses stay in primary denominators. Any repair receives only the original request, original output and actionable compiler diagnostics. Repairs cannot relax observations and are reported separately. No Codex correction of a generator's mechanism counts as a generation success.

The small adapter uses v3 provenance validation and partial-function families, plus v4's restricted expression parser. It adds the observed local row only when parent and child facts are known; it never fills an unobserved row without an explicit generated constraint/equation. Accepted complete equations are exhaustively enumerated. A natural-story counterfactual may remain unresolved or unavailable. Background conditions and nontargeted equations stay fixed during interventions. Family coverage and sampled versus exhaustive status are reported.

## Prose propositions and their actual tests

A proposition record quotes the generated description and names its type and operands. Necessity of input A for output 1 means **every** row with output 1 has A=1, including all combinations of other inputs. It is not inferred from failure at the all-zero row. Sufficiency quantifies over every completion of its antecedent. An exact conjunction requires each conjunct's necessity and their joint sufficiency. An inhibition claim distinguishes a strict possible inhibitory effect from global non-increase; the latter requires every paired context plus at least one strict witness. Exact-rule claims compare every truth-table entry. Vacuous satisfaction and untested/missing prose translations are reported, not counted as semantic validation.

Mutation tests replace conjunction by disjunction, remove a necessary factor and reverse inhibition. Each is checked against the original substantive proposition and must yield a concrete counterexample. Additional tests cover false necessity that passes the obsolete all-zero check, partial rows, provenance, factual contradictions and modular interventions. Deterministic checks establish mathematical agreement with a formalized proposition; Codex audits whether that proposition expresses the actual prose. This is not independent human validation.

## Analysis, fixed before outputs

Report source-support errors, failed/invalid constructions, factual reconstruction, controlled intervention correctness, direct answers, description/function contradictions and natural-story uncertainty separately. Include failed synthesis in the denominator; successful-only descriptions are secondary. Inspect semantic variable alignment after outputs without editing those outputs. Report missing/ambiguous alignments. Compare functional behavior over all tractable interventions on aligned variables; graph isomorphism and identifier equality are unnecessary. Twelve questions are clustered within three stories; no significance test or misleading question-level confidence interval is planned.

Codex conducts an item-level source and semantic audit against the complete short texts, marking facts, inferred mechanisms and added assumptions. Its judgments, alignment decisions and limitations are retained. Independent researcher review remains pending. Figures identify whether they show an unmodified generated model or an author reference. Displayed equations and interventions must reproduce from the same frozen record. No reader-comprehension claim is made.

## Reproduction

From `GoT-war/`, run `python3 scripts/simple_stories.py`. This offline command verifies the freeze and archived requests, rebuilds partial/complete families, reruns scoring and mutations, and renders the three SVG/PDF story figures plus the comparison table. Exact curated requests, outputs, audits and resource records are retained in `records.json.xz`; raw service logs are local. `--generate` explicitly starts requests against an already running project endpoint; it is never used by offline reproduction and refuses closed budgets or overwrite of first attempts.
