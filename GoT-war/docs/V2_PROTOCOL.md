# Version 2 experimental protocol

Status: inference is frozen only when `configs/v2_frozen_protocol.json` exists. Its timestamp, input/code hashes and gate hash are authoritative. Version 1 remains a separate preserved experiment; aggregate v1/v2 scores are not paired evidence.

## Design and access separation

The generating manifest contains 120 independent Boolean SCMs: 24 development, 24 calibration and 72 held-out test SCMs. Each split balances complete rules, one missing intermediate rule, one missing outcome rule, and two missing rules, across eight- and twelve-variable sizes. Graphs, factual backgrounds, rule choices, omissions and queries are seeded before inference. There are no paraphrases; any later paraphrase would remain with its generating SCM. The explicit texts disclose all dependencies and factual states but withhold selected exact functions. This is controlled text reasoning, not an evaluation of open-domain concept discovery.

The complete generating model, compatible-family answers and relevance labels remain outside every inference and repair prompt. Questions include single and joint interventions and factual controls. Test has 1,080 questions within 72 independent SCMs: 129 identified outcome changes, 729 identified preserved outcomes and 222 underidentified questions. The outcome-omission stratum has no identified changing interventions under the deliberately broad factual-row-compatible family; intermediate and multiple omissions supply such cases. Both factual outcome states occur (40 zero and 32 one SCMs). These counts describe a generator audit, not method performance or output-based selection.

The family conditions on fixed declared parents, active dependence, deterministic Boolean functions and the observed row. Multiple missing functions are enumerated jointly. Implementation bounds are 256 local tables and 512 global candidates, with explicit exact/sampled coverage. The present two-parent, at-most-two-missing design is exhaustive within that family. No posterior probability is assigned to candidate frequency.

## Paired components

B0 receives the source text directly. B1 receives the identical text plus generated adjacency, including usable adjacency from an equation candidate that fails formal validation. B2 executes one accepted synthesized model. ALT executes its compatible mechanisms. FULL has the same outcome answers as ALT and adds coalition attribution, source priorities and prose; those additions have separate metrics. UNIFORM replaces evidence weights only in the priority calculation. CARRY preserves the factual outcome. ORACLE_FAMILY receives the declared compatible family; ORACLE_TRUE has extra hidden generating information and is only a point-prediction diagnostic.

One fixed Qwen3-14B-AWQ revision uses seed 2701, temperature 0.2, top-p 0.8 and no thinking for the controlled tasks. Baselines have a 2,048-token cap. Synthesis has a 4,096-token cap and at most two repairs: one local node replacement and one fresh reconstruction. Repairs see source text and candidate factual-row diagnostics only. Final records keep rejected candidates and stage failures. Development records previously used for repair design are labeled as such; they are not held-out evidence. Calibration is separate and does not create a probability calibration claim.

## Analysis fixed before test

Primary denominators retain every planned test SCM, including failed synthesis. Questions are averaged within SCM. A stratum excludes only SCMs with no eligible question in that stratum. Report identified accuracy, changed/preserved accuracy, appropriate uncertainty, exact outcome sets, valid-answer coverage and unsupported confident singleton answers separately. Missing answers cannot be confident, so a low confident-error rate is not evidence of safety when coverage is low. Successful-synthesis-only estimates are secondary.

Known nonroot truth-table agreement, unknown-rule flags, endpoint factual states, directional extraction and source-rule references are separate components. Explanation generation, deterministic trace checks, reviewer ID validity, same-model support and displayability are separate. Four withheld-label reviewer controls cover a correct factual value, a flipped value, an invalid probability interpretation and explicit assumption dependence. Controls do not validate literary entailment.

Report 5,000 paired SCM bootstrap resamples (seed 271828), percentile 95% intervals and paired B1−B0, B2−B0, ALT−B2, FULL−B0 and FULL−CARRY differences. Intervals are descriptive; there is no significance-seeking continuation. Source nDCG@5 uses independently fixed affected-mechanism-slice labels (known rule 2; omitted rule statement 1; other 0). Background passages outside that slice may still help readers. No positive labels means not evaluable; an unavailable ranking with positive labels scores zero. FULL−B0/B1/UNIFORM priority differences are paired.

## Application and gate

The first-season Five Kings outcome and acquired aired-TV transcription packet are fixed in `configs/application_v2.json` and `data/manifests/v2_sources.json`. All substantive concepts, dependencies, operational revisions and functions are generated by the LLM. Deterministic software checks rather than invents story mechanisms. Source context review separates established events, directional influence and exact-function identification. Unsupported directions keep weight zero. Explicit/contextual weights 1/0.5 are declared heuristics, not probabilities. Dependent episode transcriptions do not supply independent corroboration.

The development log in `V2_METHODOLOGY.md` preserves failed fresh construction and every protocol departure. One separately labeled conditional diagnostic starts from the first canonical v1 LLM-generated variable/dependency proposal, never its old functions or evidence scores. It regenerates operational records and functions against the new transcript context. It cannot establish fresh concept discovery. Fresh completion is preferred; otherwise the conditional result is labeled as such. Selection never uses outcomes, shares or source scores. `configs/application_selection_v2.json` records the final completion-based choice.

Accepted finite application functions expose complete tables, active/inhibitory/redundant/joint behavior, generated descriptions and behavior-derived descriptions. LLM-named edge retractions require full-table invariance and source-referenced reasons. No unexplained residual may force factual fit. Global alternatives and their limits are chosen before outcomes and allocations, deduplicated functionally, and compared only on aligned paths or shared queries. At most eight paths define exact shared-edge coalition games. Declared, factual-null and domain-endpoint references and four-versus-eight-path collections are reported separately. No equation changes during intervention execution.

The technical gate verifies the four distinct controlled information configurations, rejected/retained synthesis failures, accepted-model numerical reproduction, behavior/description consistency, actual sentence IDs and actual-host process supervision. It does not certify source validity. The failed missing-intermediate pilot remains a failed end-to-end case. Application failure must remain visible rather than being repaired through researcher-written story rules.

## Resource and release limits

The immutable v2 budget allows 24 elapsed hours, targets 22 and prohibits heavy jobs in the final four hours. The measured pilot fixes a 1.1-million-output-token ceiling; optional conditional refinement reserves 800,000 tokens for the controlled campaign. Finish when the fixed evidence is complete. Checkpoint and synchronize every 20 minutes; the watchdog verifies process identity and stops only registered project jobs. No new pod, billing changes, external paid inference, push or submission. Release code, configuration, concise results, vectors and manuscript within 250 files, 8 MiB total and 2 MiB per file. Raw prompts, sources, failed candidates and weights remain local and ignored.

The conditional development gate permits one final AST serialization request for an exhausted invariant-table candidate whose own generated description asserts conditional behavior. This compiles the existing hypothesis without choosing a researcher-written equation, revising variables or changing evidence scores. The same formal/source checks apply and description-behavior agreement must be affirmative. No additional serialization repair is permitted. Failure is reported as an application-gate failure; independently validated controlled components remain evaluable.

A stored serialization response may be replayed through the documented lossless literal-operand parser: only already-generated JSON numeric/Boolean literals and exact declared category strings become constant leaves. No predicate, category value, operator or dependency is inferred. This is syntax recovery, with original/normalized forms retained and no second mechanism-generation request.
