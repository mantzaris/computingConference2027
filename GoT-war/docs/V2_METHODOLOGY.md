# Version 2 methodology and departures from version 1

Research question: **Can generative AI synthesize evidence-grounded structural models that support useful, assumption-sensitive explanations of individual and combined causal pathways?**

Version 1 is preserved at the commit and artifact paths in `configs/versions.json`. Its numerical results remain version 1 results. `V2_DIAGNOSIS.md` connects the verified weaknesses to their causes. Version 2 has its own immutable deadline, inference records, outputs and test freeze. A change in method is not evidence of improvement; only the measured paired comparisons support such a claim.

## Three evidence judgments

For a variable, retain an operational definition, domain, actor, actual event time, statement/information time, factual state and references. A narrative event, a report that an event occurred, and a character's belief are different variables. Dialogue establishes an utterance; it does not automatically establish the truth of its content.

For each candidate influence, inspect the relevant passage context and separately record support for both endpoint events, directional influence, exact function identification, contradictions, temporal and information constraints, assumptions, provenance and unresolved questions. Mere chronology/co-occurrence is not directional support. A claim that a parent affects a child is weaker than a claim that it is necessary, sufficient, substitutable or inhibitory in every context.

The fixed display rubric assigns **1** to explicitly supported direction, **0.5** to contextually supported direction with a stated passage-based rationale, and **0** to unsupported/unassessed direction. Unsupported endpoint claims, absent evidence references, contradictions, or unresolved temporal/information constraints force zero. The exact equation's identification status is recorded separately and is never converted into an invented causal probability. Unknown functional form does not itself negate otherwise supported direction. Conversely, a proposed alternative equation cannot rescue an unsupported connection. These are heuristic display weights, not calibrated probabilities or independent human judgments.

The primary source acquisition is bounded to ten first-season television episode transcript pages, at most one hour of acquisition and 30 seconds per request. ScreenScripts supplies attributed dialogue and stage directions, but its transcription fidelity and annotations are not independently authenticated. Alternative transcriptions and summaries of the same episode are dependent records of the same fictional event. A pilot screenplay draft is excluded without an aired-version concordance check. Full text stays in ignored local/private files; public manifests provide URLs, hashes, offsets and limitations. The model receives contextual passages, never analyst-supplied story causes.

## Generated structure and inspectable functions

The LLM discovers substantive variables, definitions, dependencies, equations, assumptions, alternatives, revisions and explanations. Deterministic software validates the restricted AST and its declared parents/backgrounds, types, domains, active dependence, DAG structure and factual compatibility. No unrestricted generated code is executed.

Every accepted function receives a behavior-derived description. Small finite domains are enumerated; larger domains receive labeled bounded probes. Reports expose local input/output rows, contexts where each parent changes the child, necessary and sufficient input conditions, joint enabling, substitution and inhibitory/mixed effects. Statements about these tables describe a model, not historical necessity. Original generated descriptions remain available for discrepancy analysis and are not silently treated as faithful paraphrases of an equation.

Source-supported representation revisions may alter a variable definition, parent set or explicitly justified background context. The protocol does not require every story event to be Boolean or forbid all nonroot background inputs. Background variables require operational definitions, provenance and a stated reason beyond matching the factual row; unrestricted residuals are prohibited. Targeted repair is bounded and its failures are retained. Each accepted model is frozen during intervention execution, with the same factual background reused across worlds.

## Alternatives and partial identification

In the controlled benchmark, enumerate all active deterministic Boolean functions on the declared inputs consistent with recorded factual rows, jointly across unresolved rules. This generalizes version 1's single two-parent special case. The implementation also supports bounded finite categorical/three-parent cases and reports when local or Cartesian coverage is sampled. Coverage is exhaustive only within the stated graph/domain/background/function family, never across arbitrary hidden variables or confounding.

Literary mechanisms are retained as evidence-compatible generated hypotheses with explicit assumptions and behavioral signatures. Compare intervention outcomes and functions, not wording or generation counts. Do not treat sampling frequency as a posterior. Aligned schemas permit corresponding-path sensitivity envelopes; differing schemas require common outcome queries or explicit mechanism-group alignment. One interpretation is an exploratory hypothesis, not agreement across alternatives.

## Path aggregation and evidence priority

Preserve the version 1 union-of-edges coalition game with explicit inactive-edge reference inputs. Shared edges execute once. Report every coalition outcome for small games, minimal full-outcome coalitions, unanimity, substitution, negative marginal effects, pairwise interaction ranges and allocation ties. Equal allocations may arise from conjunction, redundancy or other symmetries; equality alone does not identify the game.

For an intervention explanation, change only targeted equations and freeze everything else. Selected path edges compare counterfactual propagation with factual edge references. If path truncation leaves active omitted routes, the coalition reference need not reproduce the entire factual world: report that distinction. Application reference scenarios, selected path counts and compatible mechanisms receive separate sensitivity analyses. These allocations are not event effects, natural path-specific effects, probabilities or objective responsibility.

Passage priority sums absolute path contributions times weakest-link **directional** support and a declared allocation over supporting evidence clusters. Exact-function uncertainty appears in model envelopes and assumption views. Display influential weak-evidence paths separately. All-zero scores mean that the ranking is unavailable; alphabetical zero ties are never promoted as a substantive ranking.

## Newly frozen evaluation

The development design has 120 independently generated SCMs: 24 development, 24 calibration, 72 test; sizes 8 and 12; complete, missing intermediate, missing outcome and multiple missing rule conditions. All mechanisms use general conjunction, redundancy, XOR, inhibition or NAND families and random DAGs with shared causes. Two rules are omitted in the multiple condition. Explicit deterministic rule texts retain exactly the declared information. No uncontrolled paraphrases or repeated seeds are added to the main design.

Omissions are chosen from graph structure before method inference. Questions enumerate every non-outcome single-state flip, three seeded joint flips and three factual-value controls. No direct outcome override enters the primary queries. The gold-family audit defines identified/underidentified and changed/preserved strata without consulting method outputs. Missing-outcome records with only a factual rule row need not have identified changing interventions; this remaining limitation is explicitly reported rather than hidden. Both factual states and identified changes occur elsewhere in the design. Splits group generating SCMs and all repeated questions.

Paired methods see the same source text: B0 direct answers; B1 source plus the synthesized graph without equations; B2 execution of the first admissible synthesized model; ALT execution over compatible alternatives; FULL uses ALT's answers plus inspected mechanisms, coalition explanation, evidence ranking and prose/review. FULL and ALT share their structural answers by design. Factual carry-forward is a diagnostic baseline. Oracle generating-model and declared-family solvers separate hidden-world prediction from appropriate uncertainty.

Report identified accuracy, changed/preserved accuracy, underidentification handling, unsupported confident answers, synthesis/repair validity, known-function agreement, evidence grounding, source ranking, explanation fidelity, reviewer behavior, runtime and tokens separately. Failures stay in primary denominators; conditional-on-synthesis results are secondary. Confidence intervals and paired differences resample SCMs, not questions. No composite score certifies the whole pipeline.

For source ranking, an independently defined gold causal slice contains descendants of intervened variables that are ancestors of the queried outcome, excluding intervened equations. A relevant paragraph gets grade 2 for an available rule, 1 for an explicitly missing rule and 0 otherwise. This assesses structural information relevance, including useful uncertainty statements; it is not a human ranking of literary importance. Labels do not depend on generated paths. Compare FULL with uniform support and direct/graph source rankings. In this initial rule-text benchmark, documented directions are explicit even when functions are absent; it does not independently calibrate graded literary support.

## Explanation and review gates

A structured record precedes prose: exact intervention/outcome, computed states and outcome sets, mechanisms, paths, reference/full values, allocations and ties, evidence status and assumptions. The LLM explains these supplied values without recalculation. Each generated sentence receives a fixed software-assigned ID before semantic review. The reviewer must return each actual ID exactly once; invented IDs, missing sentences or quoted audit instructions are reviewer failures.

Deterministic checks cover numbers, identifiers, ties and probability terminology. Semantic review separately addresses interpretation and entailment. Reviewer format failure is distinguished from an unsupported explanation. Known factual-value and probability-interpretation control sentences assess the reviewer against controlled labels; these controls do not validate the literary model. A researcher review packet remains unadjudicated unless an actual review occurs.

The end-to-end gate covers four benchmark omission conditions and one Five Kings construction before test inference. Repairs are targeted and bounded; no unchanged broad regeneration is allowed. Freeze achievable sample size, prompts, analysis and token ceiling from measured throughput before test requests. The initial temporary pilot ceiling was 80,000 output tokens. Measured throughput set the campaign safety ceiling to 1,100,000 output tokens; it is not inherited from version 1 and is not a target to consume.

## Engineering and publication boundaries

The version 2 24-hour deadline starts conservatively before initial inspection, reserves at least four hours after heavy work, and survives restarts. The remote watchdog verifies process birth identities; unverified launches fail before registration. Only project-created jobs may be stopped. Version 2 checkpoints synchronize every 20 minutes. No new pod, paid external inference, unrelated-service restart, push or paper submission is authorized or performed.

Keep the existing 250-file, 8-MiB-total, 2-MiB-per-file staged release limits. New results, figures and manuscript are versioned in dedicated subdirectories, with curated compressed reproduction records and raw candidates retained locally. The final scientific claim must follow measured component results and the actual level of source adjudication, whether that supports an application, narrower methods or failure-analysis contribution.

## Retained development history and departures

Development clarification: the flagship outcome keeps version 1's first-season armed-conflict endpoint and removes the subsidiary clause about wider royal claims through S2E4, because the new transcript corpus ends at S1E10. The initial source-extraction records were preserved under `application/five_kings_scope_pilot`; the source-aligned construction restarts before variable/graph selection. No story-specific variable or mechanism was supplied by this clarification.

The first four-case inference pilot produced 18,001 output tokens in 378.49 request seconds (47.56 tokens/s). Its size-eight per-case cost, multiplied by 120 SCMs and a conservative 1.4 size/variation factor, projects 756,042 tokens. Adding 220,000 for the application and 100,000 for targeted repairs/limited transfer yields 1,076,042; the campaign safety ceiling is rounded to 1,100,000. At half measured throughput this requires about 12.85 inference hours, within the separate absolute deadline and four-hour delivery reserve. The ceiling is not an instruction to consume the allowance.

Development repairs are bounded and retained by round. A missing function repeatedly became an inactive constant or an expression that contradicted its own factual row. Formal diagnostics now show the candidate's evaluated factual row, and the LLM can repair a local mechanism by specifying its complete finite truth table. Deterministic software only compiles those generated entries into the restricted expression language; it does not choose entries, supply the hidden generating rule, or change source support. This tests an inspectable representation of mechanisms, rather than relying on the model to perform Boolean arithmetic in its prose. Each final-protocol case still permits at most two repairs.

Source-context packaging removes nested prior audit logs and redundant behavior witnesses from prompts while retaining operational variable interfaces, full small truth tables and bounded passage context. Large extracted inventories receive a second model-driven shortlist stage. These are declared context limits, not claims of exhaustive source attention.

The finite-table repair pilot did not solve active-dependence failures: the model repeatedly emitted constant or one-parent tables for declared two-parent dependencies. It remains a retained negative development result. Before its final gate, the selected controlled repair policy is one local expression repair followed by one fresh reconstruction, each using only source text and candidate factual-row diagnostics. This avoids repeating an unchanged failed reconstruction; no further prompt search is planned after that gate. A valid synthesized model is not required to manufacture a favorable primary denominator.

The flagship variable schema is now generated in two stages: a compact LLM concept plan, then one operational variable record per concept against retrieved passage context. This addresses measured truncation of monolithic schema outputs. The change supplies neither concepts nor equations by hand; it preserves failed monolithic generations and the source-derived inventory.

The technical gate does not require every development synthesis to succeed. It verifies all four information configurations against the declared compatible-family oracle, checks that failed synthesis is rejected and retained, and reproduces the accepted models' outcomes, coalitions and reviewed sentence IDs. The final hybrid pilot still has a failed missing-intermediate synthesis. This is explicitly not a successful end-to-end example; primary test denominators include analogous failures. The study estimates synthesis failure rather than admitting an invalid program to satisfy the gate.

Functional agreement is measured for known nonroot rules over all Boolean parent inputs, with factual backgrounds fixed. Root/background assignments contribute to factual-state grounding instead of inflating the function-agreement score. All 120 visible benchmark narratives are distinct, with no identical narrative shared across splits.

Before freezing, numerical review corrected the definition of a minimal full-outcome coalition for nonmonotone games: every strict subset is tested, because checking only immediate deletions can mislabel a three-way parity coalition. The Shapley allocations themselves are unchanged. Missing temporal/information review fields remain unresolved and receive zero directional weight. Alternative-family descriptions are regenerated from each alternative's actual function.

Explanation review receives actual source context: the full explicit rule record for controlled texts and a bounded passage context for the flagship. Full application passages remain private; their hashes and source IDs are retained in publication records. Software splits actual prose into sentence IDs before review, even when an LLM places several sentences inside one JSON text field. These checks do not establish independent source entailment.

Schema diagnostics are distinct from source judgments. The application pilot produced categorical domains with numeric factual/reference states, literal pipe-separated proposition types, and inventory IDs where passage IDs were required. Structured decoding now enforces the representation and citation namespaces. The LLM still chooses all concepts, domain meanings, factual states, references, parents and mechanisms; syntactic correctness does not earn event or directional support. The user-specified outcome is operationalized as a separate source-grounded record, rather than requiring a matching W identifier to already occur in an extracted fact inventory.

Source-ranking labels evaluate the affected-mechanism slice specified in the generator. Unaffected background passages can also help a reader resolve a query but are outside these positive labels. This restricted label definition, and the lack of independent literary relevance judgments, limit broader claims about source usefulness.

The graph gate exposed directed cycles and proposed future-to-past links. Structured decoding now allows each node every other supplied variable except itself and variables strictly later under the recorded episode times; unknown-time candidates remain eligible. This enforces a general temporal constraint rather than supplying edges or an arbitrary variable-order chain. Cycle diagnostics name the actual cycle. The graph context prioritizes the specified outcome passages so a source budget cannot silently select only an earlier chronological prefix. Any representation revision returns bounded replacements/splits, not another oversized full schema.

Application model selection is fixed before outcomes or allocations are calculated: baseline, up to eight round-robin single-function changes in reverse topological order, then bounded joint changes including the all-last-option assignment and seed-2801 combinations, at most 12 models total. This exposes some interacting unresolved functions while preserving a clear non-exhaustive coverage statement. It does not select for unequal path shares or favorable source weights.

Event establishment is also kept distinct from reference-scenario clarity. The contextual audit records both, but an unclear counterfactual reference does not itself negate an established factual event. Reference ambiguity remains an explicit interpretation limitation. This separation was implemented before any accepted flagship path scores were calculated; unsupported directional claims still receive zero, without exception.
### Bounded graph-construction correction before the test freeze

The joint parent-map generator exhausted two targeted repairs after repeatedly proposing cycles through an unresolved-time composite event. Its full and compact representation repairs also failed validation. Those candidates remain in the development records. This is a failed source-construction gate, not positive evidence of narrative causal understanding.

The final bounded correction separates a generated admissible order from per-target dependency proposals. The LLM orders the operational variables subject to recorded episode-time constraints; within-episode and unresolved-event positions remain explicit assumptions. It then selects zero to four direct parents from earlier variables, with a separate target-centered passage context. Software enforces the order and checks the resulting DAG and temporal paths. The order supplies no positive causal evidence, and every dependency still receives a separate contextual directional audit. An unsupported dependency retains zero support. This changes the generation factorization, not the support rubric. No researcher-specified story edge is inserted. The earlier joint-map implementation and failed outputs are retained for the failure analysis.


A subsequent per-target proposal cited source identifiers listed in variable metadata even though their passages were absent from that request. These proposals were rejected. The corrected contract lists admissible passage IDs explicitly and constrains decoding to the passages actually supplied. The later contextual audit still evaluates entailment; valid identifiers do not earn support by themselves.

The pre-freeze temporal audit also rejects an implicit override of missing operational event times: an application variable with `time=null` makes incident timing constraints unresolved even if a same-model reviewer returns a generic consistent label. A composite event needs an evidence-grounded representation revision to resolve this gap. This conservative check does not apply to the controlled untimed station labels; their intervention semantics are explicitly declared. It changes no equation or attribution value and never increases support.

Before freezing, the structured prose record retains source IDs without exposing internal source-allocation weights as story quantities. Deterministic review now rejects unknown generated variable IDs, checks simple explicit factual-outcome numeric assertions against the matching field, and requires an identifiable outcome term. These bounded lexical checks do not resolve general semantic entailment or arbitrary negation. The final gate refresh preserves all structural models and intervention/attribution values while rerunning only the affected explanation and reviewer requests.

The first accepted ordered graph reached mechanism generation, where the LLM failed to serialize a declared root: it emitted self-references, malformed constants, then a constant inconsistent with the declared external-input interface. These failures remain retained. The corrected application compiler serializes an LLM-declared parentless scope boundary as its own external identity input, using the already generated domain, observed state and reference. This is an explicit representation convention, labeled `not_applicable_scope_boundary`, not an inferred narrative reason or an unrestricted residual for an endogenous mechanism. All substantive nonroot functions remain LLM-generated and audited. Controlled synthesis retains its frozen explicit-text root task; no benchmark prompt is silently changed.

Typed mechanism generation exposed two further contract errors: existing parents were redeclared as background variables, and categorical inputs/outputs were treated as Boolean conditions/values. Targeted diagnostics now identify the exact namespace collision and repeat the generated input/output domains and factual row; no desired equation is supplied. After a formally accepted categorical rule was rejected by contextual review, representation repair was found to receive only a generic rejection and context dominated by other nodes. The corrected bounded repair receives the actual fallible reviewer findings, prioritizes the failed variable and its parents in retrieval, and returns field patches rather than another full schema. Changed records still require actual supplied passage references and fresh event review. Earlier reviewer judgments are excluded from that event-review prompt. These are retained development corrections, not paired evidence of better literary validity.

The revised nine-ancestor graph again failed direct AST generation when the LLM applied logical operators to categorical values. The selected application interface therefore generates full finite input-output tables for Boolean/categorical mechanisms with at most 64 input rows. Software enumerates input rows from the LLM-generated domains; the LLM chooses every output code and its assumptions. Codes are lossless domain indices, not causal quantities. A generic compiler produces the restricted typed expression and removes identical conditional branches without changing behavior. Inactive-parent, factual-row, domain, and source-compatibility checks remain unchanged. Larger or background-augmented cases retain direct AST proposals with explicit limits. Review receives losslessly coded actual truth tables to fit the model context. Original AST failures are retained. This application development change does not replace the controlled benchmark's selected AST/repair policy.

The finite-table gate resumes the retained source-derived graph, identified by its hash, without reopening variable or parent selection. No additional representation round is granted by that resume. A generated request for a source-defined background can defer to the original typed-expression interface; the background must still be independently operationalized and source-referenced and must pass the no-factual-patch audit. This preserves the possibility of substantive background context rather than silently restricting every model to the displayed parents.

The finite-table gate exposed a substantive representation inconsistency: repeated generated functions were constant across the two categorical states of a proposed parent. Forcing that parent to be active would impose a dependency the generated behavior rejected. The final application contract therefore allows the LLM to name provisional parents for retraction, with a reason and actual passage references. Software verifies invariance over every supplied finite input row for each named retraction; it does not select the edges. Every retained parent must still be active. All variants share the same accepted retraction so their paths remain aligned. A variable with no remaining parents keeps its generated constant observed-state function as an explicit scope boundary with unexplained upstream causes; no residual is inserted. Removing all outcome ancestry is a construction failure, not an explanatory model. Retraction is considered before any outcome or attribution is computed, is independently visible in the mechanism audit, and never changes the directional-support rubric.

A separately labeled conditional refinement uses the first canonical retained version-1 Five Kings LLM proposal as its initialization. No researcher-written story variable or edge is introduced. The old equations, mechanism descriptions and evidence scores are withheld from generation. The LLM regenerates operational definitions, domains, factual/reference states and citations from the new transcript context, then proposes new functions; the inherited adjacency remains an unverified hypothesis subject to explicit retraction and source audit. This is a diagnostic of refinement conditional on an earlier generated representation, not evidence that fresh version-2 discovery succeeded. The fresh attempt remains preserved and separately reported. Neither construction is selected using outcomes, shares or source scores; fresh construction is the flagship if it completes the technical gate, otherwise the conditional result is labeled as such. This diagnostic reserves 800,000 output tokens for the fixed controlled campaign and uses no second model.

The conditional refinement's outcome table also exhausted two repairs: its prose described conditional influence while all 16 outputs were one.
