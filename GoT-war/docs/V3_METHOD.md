## Inspectable representation

The LLM first proposes operational variables and **edge mechanisms**, with a substantive process, concise label, participants/resources/information, operating conditions, exceptions, source-specific rationales, contradictions, gaps, status, functional implications and warranted alternatives. Endpoint event support, causal direction and functional evidence are distinct. Multiple parents require a joint-process explanation. Variables distinguish facts, reports and beliefs, event versus statement time, and explicit boundary contexts versus unknown endogenous functions. There is no node minimum, forced connectivity, causal vocabulary specific to a story, or rule that chronology establishes direction.

Every evidence reference selects a passage and a source-exact quote. Software resolves character offsets and copies the source URL/line/episode location into the record. Exact matching establishes provenance only. Semantic source review inspects the available passage context and the stated inferential bridge. Reviews are by the same model and remain fallible. No confidence becomes a probability. No full copyrighted transcript is redistributed.

Construction is: passages → propositions/qualifications → representation diagnosis and targeted retrieval → bounded justified revision → functional constraints → compatible functions. Revision records retain the question, retrieved passage IDs, before/after hashes, operations and justification. Allowed operations include splitting composites, separating permission/action/outcome or report/event, adding a supported mediator, correcting scope, retracting a link, proposing alternative structure, and recording an unresolved gap. A structural revision is a proposal, not evidence of improved validity.

## Mathematical contract

For finite variables, `X_j = f_j(Pa_j,U_j)` with

`F_j(E,A) = { f : D(Pa_j,U_j) → D(X_j) | f satisfies C_E and C_A }`.

Each constraint has a stable identifier, a formal predicate, origin (`evidence` or `assumption`), rationale and references. Unknown rows stay unknown. A factual row is applied only if explicitly proposed with provenance; software never invents unobserved rows. Non-target boundary equations retain their observed inputs across worlds. A root variable's equation can be replaced by an intervention; separately declared background parameters cannot be targeted and remain fixed. An input must be represented as a parent variable to study its intervention. A parent need not be active in every compatible function: a proposed dependence is not universal relevance. A help/enabling statement can justify an existential change claim without universal necessity, sufficiency or monotonicity. Hidden confounding and unrepresented factors remain outside the declared family.

The finite engine supports allowed output sets on full/partial input contexts, necessary conditions, existential directional witnesses, monotonicity, and invariance. Partial-context row constraints quantify over all unspecified inputs and therefore require an explicit rationale. All compatible tables are enumerated when the space fits the declared limit. Otherwise bounded candidate search is labeled sampled, including an empty sample: it is never proof of inconsistency. Cartesian combinations preserve one fixed function per variable across factual/intervention worlds. Exhaustive singleton outcome sets identify answers **within the declared graph, domains and constraints**, not the narrative universe. Sampled singletons are observations, not identification. The result also records inner/outer outcome bounds. Two limited certificates are implemented separately from sampling agreement: a direct intervention on the target fixes its value by modularity; valid witnesses attaining every value in the target domain exhaust its possible outcome set. Other sampled agreements remain uncertified. An empty certified family is inconsistent; an absent executable scope or empty sample yields unavailable evaluation. Unmodeled background is not filled with a residual.

Checks are separate: A, source→description (contextual semantic audit); B, description→function (reviewer-translated structured assertions plus exhaustive finite counterexamples or labeled probes); C, function→reported result (deterministic execution/identifier/value checks). Claims are checked against an independently compiled family and do not automatically constrain it. A failed check causes a bounded targeted revision or withholding; original substantive prose is never silently replaced by generic text. Unsupported assumptions remain displayed as assumptions. Unknown functions and source-unresolved descriptions can survive as partial hypotheses without a complete intervention answer.

## Bounded development comparison

Three initial cases: two newly authored controlled narratives (explicit substitution/inhibition; implied process with an omitted intermediate mechanism and misleading chronology) and one natural first-season television packet. The natural outcome is armed conflict between the Stark-led northern/Riverlands coalition and Lannister-controlled crown being active at the end of season 1, not onset or a particular victory. Generic lexical retrieval constructs its fixed available packet from the cached transcripts; the LLM may retrieve within that same packet. A pre-inference packet audit found that unstratified lexical retrieval omitted the endpoint. The final policy allocates 2,600 characters to each of the early/middle/late episode strata (4/3/3 episodes), using only the outcome as query and retaining source-native scenes. No method outputs informed this revision. All arms have identical packet access and outcome. No researcher supplies story causes or equations.

A runs the existing v2 `Construction` methods using a version-isolated adapter and fresh records. Its unchanged source prompts include television wording; applying them to controlled narratives is a disclosed domain mismatch. B uses exactly A's retained representation/functions and only generates edge/joint presentation. Hash checks reject changes to A. If A fails, B cannot manufacture a model and inherits that failure. C uses mechanism-first partial synthesis and revisions. Per-case output allowances: A 20,000; B increment 3,000 (shared A cost reported); C 24,000. Maximum planned generation is 141,000, with a 150,000 global hard cap and two-hour inference-service lifetime from launch, whichever comes first. Setup before service launch does not consume service lifetime. No new infrastructure or paid inference. No extension to fill the budget.

Seed 3701, temperature 0.2; at most one representation revision and one consistency repair in C; v2 retains its two artifact repairs subject to the case cap. Failed generations are retained. The first three cases are the primary denominator. Expansion is optional only if all three C records have traceable end-to-end output, unknowns survive, no accepted description/function contradiction remains, and controlled queries can be aligned and executed; this is a development gate, not a claim of scientific validity. Do not expand after gate failure.

Metrics are reported by case and component: construction/coverage/failure, source and temporal review flags, known behavior agreement, uncertainty preservation, controlled intervention outcome-set accuracy and unsupported singletons, mechanism/function counterexamples, repairs, tokens and time. Controlled query descriptions and references are frozen before inference; gold equations/answers are excluded from every construction, audit and repair prompt. Query alignment may abstain and remains auditable. Natural judgments have no independent gold. No significance claims, reader-comprehension claim, or extrapolation from three cases. The old held-out set remains unchanged.

Graphs show only generated phrases passing the display gate, with visible evidence status and record IDs; unresolved hypotheses are separated from accepted functional descriptions. Joint-mechanism annotations refer to the catalogue. The catalogue retains citations, assumptions, unknown behavior, counterexamples and withheld descriptions. Zero attribution does not erase explanation relevance; no new source-weighting advantage is claimed.

## Positioning

[PAL (Gao et al., 2023)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/gao23f.html) already separates language-model program synthesis from deterministic execution for symbolic tasks. That division is not a v3 novelty. Its reported task results do not establish causal identification from narratives.

[Li, Pan and Pi, arXiv:2504.07459v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07459v2) combines event extraction, linguistic features, STAC categories and iterative graph refinement, including causal/chronological distinctions and missing-link investigation. V3 does not claim those ideas as new. The narrower prototype evaluates persistent mechanism propositions, partial executable families and cross-layer disagreement records. Their graph-quality evaluation is different from verifying modular interventions or partially identified outcome sets. No priority claim or empirical superiority over either paper is made.

The next experiment must follow the measured bottleneck, with independent item-level adjudication before claims of grounded natural-narrative discovery.
