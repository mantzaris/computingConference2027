It lists anonymous PDF submission, an 18-page main-text limit with up to 7 additional pages for references/appendices, and a 150–250-word abstract. Figures and tables must remain legible within the page margins. The downloaded official LaTeX archive and its SHA-256 are recorded in `paper/template/SOURCE.json`.

The current call lists 15 October 2026 for round 1 and 1 December 2026 for round 2, both Anywhere on Earth. These dates are context for the researcher, not an instruction to submit.

The conference requires disclosure of generative-AI assistance and human responsibility for scientific ideas, arguments, results and conclusions. The delivered document is therefore explicitly an unsubmitted, AI-assisted manuscript scaffold. Its declaration does not assert human review or author approval. The researcher must adjudicate the scientific interpretation and determine compliance before any submission. No paper has been submitted by this project.
